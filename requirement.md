# Task requirements

Version: 1.1 | Updated: 2026-11-22

Only internships (including praktik/LIA) and fresher/graduate UX, UI/UX, interaction and digital product-design roles are in scope. Fresher means the employer accepts candidates with no professional UX experience; a junior title alone is insufficient. Exclude roles requiring prior professional UX experience, mid-level, senior, lead and management roles. When requirements are unclear, mark pending verification rather than a match. Onsite/hybrid roles must be in Skåne; elsewhere require explicit fully remote eligibility from Sweden. Keep student/LIA conditions visible.

Before searching job boards, work through the company career-page queue: Skåne companies first, then the remaining master list. Resolve missing career URLs through official company sites or targeted web search. For every company, record URL, check date, scope of review and outcome (checked, no relevant role, blocked, URL unresolved, or partial). A blocked page is not evidence of no vacancies. Finish or explicitly account for each queued company before opening job boards; if execution limits interrupt the pass, save the remaining queue and resume company checks before job boards. Only after this pass use job boards for additional discovery, then verify every promising lead at the employer and add new companies to the registry. Employer-owned ATS pages count as company career pages.

Aim for up to 100 newest distinct in-scope roles, ordered by source-reported date. Report the actual count and any shortfall; never add experienced or unrelated roles to meet a quota. Preserve date provenance and separate employer verification from provider metadata. See workflow.md for the operating steps and company registry rules.

Produce a clean Excel workbook with a concise Open jobs sheet, a Reviewed roles audit table, CV and portfolio actions, and compact coverage notes. Use filterable tables, frozen headers, readable widths, typed dates, clickable links and editable application status. Avoid blank formatted columns and duplicate rows. Keep closed jobs out of Open jobs.

For each shortlisted role provide ready-to-use truthful tailored CV wording and exactly three main knowledge/evidence-gap bullets, without sub-bullets. Keep engineering and UX experience distinct. Pomodoro Platform is the sole ongoing UX case study; do not claim it is shipped or validated.

Version control: preserve originals under docs/v1.0/ with a manifest and SHA-256 hashes. Record unavailable originals honestly. Never label generated text or a live URL as an original cover letter or a full portfolio archive. Keep exactly two document directories: `docs/v1.0/` preserves originals unchanged; `docs/v1.1/` contains the current working documents and is updated in place. Git commits and PRs retain revision history. Do not create additional document-version directories unless the user requests them. Dated scan reports continue to use `reports/PR_<number>_YYYY-MM-DD/`. Maintain requirement.md, memory.md, CV improve.md, CHANGELOG.md and a names-only company_list.txt.

Each weekly run creates a branch and a pull request. After GitHub assigns its number, use PR_<number>_YYYY-MM-DD for the PR title and reports directory, with Europe/Stockholm date. Commit CSV/JSON alongside Excel so changes are reviewable. Preserve prior reports and application statuses by stable job ID. Merge only after validation; user authorized repository management on 2026-11-22. Never apply for jobs or contact employers.

Schedule: Friday 09:00 Europe/Stockholm, no end date. ChatGPT automation executes the scan; this repository is the versioned destination, not an independently running GitHub Actions scraper. Verify task run state, commit, PR and workbook before reporting success. On failure, report the stage and actual error, preserve progress, and never fabricate output.

Maintain companies/registry.csv as the source of company career URLs and location evidence. Derive company_list_skane.txt only from evidenced Skåne offices; preserve unresolved legacy companies in the master list with pending status. Expand verified company coverage each run. Log every company check and any unprocessed queue before job-board discovery.
