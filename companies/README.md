# Company monitoring directory

Updated 2026-11-22: **200 master entries**, increased from 175, and **33 employers with Skåne office evidence**. The master preserves historical discoveries; unverified legacy entries are explicitly pending, not endorsed matches.

The registry records official evidence links and the date the route was reviewed. These are route/location checks, not a completed search of every vacancy. 34 employer routes were researched (33 Skåne plus Stratsys); 166 legacy entries await route verification. Two Skåne companies have no resolved general career index; their official evidence pages are retained separately. Sinch's ATS returned no readable body and remains blocked/dynamic. Search-index and direct-open content may differ in freshness; recheck at each scan.

Do not assume the unclassified companies are outside Skåne. The 33-company subset is verified coverage so far, not an exhaustive geographic classification of all 200 entries. Expand it when evidence becomes available. Entries include public-sector employers as well as private companies.

Use official career/office pages to establish location. Offices in Copenhagen or Älmhult alone do not qualify as Skåne. A remote employer without a local office belongs only in the master list. General application forms and internship-program pages are watchlist leads, not open vacancies.

For every scan save a company_checks.csv beside the report, with company, career_url, checked_at, pages_or_filters_reviewed, outcome, matching_job_ids and next_action. Save an unprocessed queue if interrupted. Account for the company queue before job-board searches, as described in workflow.md.
