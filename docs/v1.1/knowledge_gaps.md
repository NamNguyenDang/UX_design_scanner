# Role-specific knowledge and evidence gaps

Version 1.1 | 2026-11-22. Exactly three main bullets per role. These are proposed learning/evidence actions, not claims of inability. Eligibility checks are separate in the workbook.

## Gaddr

- Responsive components and states: show a Pomodoro component set with mobile/desktop layouts and empty/error states; current CV does not evidence this depth.
- Testing-to-iteration evidence: document a genuine usability observation and the resulting Pomodoro revision; do not invent participants or outcomes.
- Developer handoff: annotate interaction rules and acceptance criteria for one Pomodoro flow; shipped SaaS work remains unproven.

## Learnpoint

- Agentic workflows: the JD expects design-tool and AI-agent proficiency; build a small documented workflow with human review before claiming this skill.
- End-to-end feature research: turn one Pomodoro need into a research question, prototype and evaluated revision, with your contribution explicit.
- Public explanation: practise a short case-study article and presentation that connect findings to design decisions; evidence is not yet included in the CV.

## Nordhealth

- Human–AI interaction: demonstrate uncertainty, error recovery and user control in a clearly labeled proposed Pomodoro interaction exercise.
- Evaluation evidence: document how usability observations change a prototype; AI-specific testing and trust research are not demonstrated in the CV.
- Accessible information design: show readable data, keyboard focus and clear status messages in one flow; current CV lacks explicit accessibility evidence.

- Motion and interaction evidence: create a small purposeful Pomodoro microinteraction and explain its behavior; animation is a bonus skill, not a confirmed current strength.
