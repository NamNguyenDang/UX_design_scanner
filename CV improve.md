Add a clear project link near the top of the CV. Use actual evidence only; keep untested ideas labeled as proposals.
- **Demonstrate implementation-ready interface skills.** Show responsive Figma components, variants and empty/error/loading states, then document accessibility checks and developer handoff. Your CV lists Figma and prototyping, but does not yet demonstrate this depth. For AI-focused roles, add a small proposed interaction exercise covering uncertainty and user control.
- **Make your transition and communication evidence precise.** Lead with junior UI/UX capability, relevant coursework and graphic-design work; retain engineering as transferable requirements and stakeholder experience. Keep English B2 and Swedish B1 accurate. Show a brief English design presentation or case-study write-up, and confirm student status, availability and working conditions separately from learning needs.
